What do the metrics mean?
RMSE
Average distance between estimate and truth. Lower is better.
NEES
Is the filter's covariance honest about its real error? Ideal ≈ number of states. Above the interval: optimistic (overconfident). Below: pessimistic.
NIS
Same idea using the innovations (measurement − prediction); needs no truth. Ideal ≈ measurement size.
